rclone (personal configuration)

A personal, single-user tool configuration for syncing files with the owner's own Google Drive.

About this app

This site describes a private OAuth client configuration named "rclone", created and used by one individual only. It is used together with the open-source rclone command-line program so the owner can download, back up, and sync files stored in his own Google Drive account, on his own computers. The app is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not provide any service to third parties.

Privacy policy

What data is accessed. With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses files in the owner's own Google Drive (scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive), solely so the owner can transfer and manage his own files.

How data is used. Files are transferred only between Google Drive and the owner's own machines, at the owner's direction. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine (in rclone's configuration file). No data is collected on any server, no data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and no data is used for advertising or analytics.

Data retention and deletion. Tokens remain only on the owner's local machine and can be deleted at any time by removing the local configuration. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from his Google Account's third-party access page.
